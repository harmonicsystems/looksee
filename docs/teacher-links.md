# One bookmark for the whole day

For the classroom Chromebook. Open this link once and bookmark it (the star in the address bar,
or Ctrl + D). It is named **🏫🔤😊 School day · EN**. All day, the screen follows the clock: when a
part of the day begins, its words start at the next slide. Press Play to start them; nothing
plays by itself.

# Or one bookmark for each part of the day

For the classroom Chromebook. Open each link, then bookmark it (the star in the address bar,
or Ctrl + D). Each bookmark is named for you, so the bookmarks bar reads like the day. One click
on a bookmark switches the screen to those words; the Play button starts them.

| when | bookmark name | link |
|---|---|---|
| 7:50 Arrival | 🏫 Good morning · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=arrival |
| 9:14 Specials | 🏫 Lining up · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=lineUp |
| 9:56 ELA | 🏫 Core words · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=core |
| Snack | 🏫 Asking · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=requesting |
| 11:20 Math | 🏫 Math words · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=mathWords |
| 12:05 Lunch | 🏫 Asking, Five senses · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=requesting.senses |
| 2:00 Going home | 🏫 Going home · EN | https://harmonicsystems.github.io/looksee/?v=1&sets=packUp |

- A change made while a bookmark is open (Spanish words, slower slides) is not saved into the
  bookmark by itself. To keep it, bookmark the page again and replace the old one.
- The videos (Good Morning Song, Count to 100, Goodbye Song, an aquarium) are not in yet. They
  will appear under Tools ▸ Watch with these words around them.
- The words are a first try. Tell us what to add, remove or change in each list.
