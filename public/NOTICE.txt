Emoji pictures in Looksee
=========================

The picture files in the feelings and people folders are unmodified copies
of Google's Noto Emoji:

  <code>.avif   the animated emoji, 512 x 512
  <code>.svg    the still emoji

Animated Noto Emoji is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0).
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
  Source:  https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/

The still images are Noto Emoji artwork. The Noto Emoji project states that
its tools and most image resources are under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
  License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  Source:  https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji

The copies were fetched from https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/latest/
by scripts/fetch-pictures.mjs. Looksee does not contact Google while it runs;
it shows these copies.

The emoji on the school-day bar are not files. They are drawn by the device's
own emoji font.
